# PFA Interactive Research Workflows V1

## PFA Setup Coach

PFA Setup Coach converts existing point-in-time pattern and sequence notification states into an explanatory feed. It prioritizes sequences in `Watching` or `ResearchEligible` state and patterns in `Detected` or `ResearchEntryEligible` state. Every card exposes the instrument, knowledge clock, current role, next watched role, and the source lifecycle message.

The main application polls these versioned research-notification APIs every 30 seconds. When a newer state becomes available during the same browser session, an in-app notice points the user back to the coach feed. The coach never invents a probability, calls a negative hypothesis profitable, recommends an entry, activates a strategy, or routes an order.

## Pattern Intelligence

Registered pattern modules are keyboard- and pointer-openable. Their detail view contains:

- module ID, version, maturity, supported timeframes, and research authority;
- a code-native visual example of the structure;
- a plain-language description of what the detector records;
- explicit disclosure when the module is planned and does not emit evidence.

Individual detected setups continue to open the deeper point-in-time research record containing geometry, scenario policies and prices, outcome classifications, and sequence membership.

## Research and Evidence

Comparable setup rows open an explanation of their instrument/timeframe/pattern grouping and link into individual detections. Daily discovery events open their exact clock, strength, evidence calculation, OHLC context, and research-only interpretation. Evidence classification cards open the meaning and methodology behind persistent, watchlist, negative, and insufficient-evidence groups.

## Agent & Module Center

The Agent Center remains usable in research mode even when paid activation is locked. Product-module cards open their manifest identity, runtime state, integration mode, entitlement, and capabilities. Research table rows open their complete displayed values. A dedicated Setup Coach section explains current lifecycle states using the same governed notification sources as the main application.

## Certification Sandbox

The Sandbox now has explicit refresh and retry controls. Strategy queue, telemetry, realism, readiness, decision, coverage, and intelligence rows are openable and explain their certification meaning and hard safety boundaries. An empty strategy queue or zero simulated accounts is a valid gated state rather than a broken page: no candidate can enter certification until the required validation and registry gates are satisfied.
